# Fly

An arcade flying game for the browser. You are a spirit bird over a Ghibli-style archipelago: green islands,
white villages, a snow-capped volcano, a floating stone ring, sea arches and cumulus clouds drifting over a
turquoise sea. Fly fast, ride the wind, and find the twelve golden feathers. Or pick **Challenge** and see how long your light lasts.

**Play:** https://stokres.github.io/fly/

## How to play

- **Fly anywhere.** Diving trades height for speed; skimming low over water or grass refills your energy.
- **Light motes** hang in golden trails. Fly through them for energy; each one plays the next note of a melody.
- **Wind currents** (white streaks of wind) grab you and carry you at high speed: through the sea arch, up
  around the volcano, through the ring.
- **Shrines** stand on high places. Fly through one to reveal where a golden feather is hidden.
- **Golden feathers** (12) are in special places. Each one makes your boost stronger. Find them all.

## Challenge mode

Pick **Challenge** (Reto) on the title screen. You have three lights.

- **Shadow spirits** hunt you. They are faster than you but turn wider: out-turn them, dive away, or
  **ram them while boosting** to purify them (points and energy). Touched without boosting, you lose a light.
- **Crashing** into the ground in a steep dive, or into rock at speed, also costs a light.
- Every half minute or so, **something happens**: a lightning storm (get out of the glowing columns before the
  bolt falls), a ring rush against the clock, a golden spirit to chase down, a shower of falling stars, or a
  swarm of shadows. Clear an event to win a light back.
- Everything scores; chaining actions builds a multiplier up to ×8. Flying low and fast scores too.
  Danger rises every minute or so. The music picks up drums when things get tense.

## Controls

| Action | Mouse + keyboard | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | Mouse (click the game to capture it) or W/S, A/D | Left stick |
| Flap | Space or left click | A / RT |
| Boost | Shift or right click | B / LT |
| Pause | Esc | |
| Mute | M | |

**On a phone** (hold it upright, portrait): tilt sideways to turn, tilt the top away to dive and toward
you to climb. The golden button boosts, ⌃ flaps, ◎ recalibrates the neutral position. Whatever angle you
hold the phone at when you press Fly becomes neutral. Without a motion sensor, drag a thumb to steer.

Settings (language, volume, mouse sensitivity, camera roll, invert, graphics quality) are in the menu.
Progress is saved in your browser.


## How it's made

- **three.js r184**, TypeScript and Vite; all rendering is custom stylized shading on top of three's materials
  (cel-ramped lighting, painted terrain, baked mountain shadows and ambient occlusion, stylized water,
  height fog, bloom and grading).
- **Every model is generated by Blender Python scripts** in `art/`: trees and rocks, clouds, the village,
  windmills, lighthouse and ruins, the landmarks, and the spirit bird. `npm run assets` rebuilds them
  (needs Blender 4).
- Sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API (wind, wingbeats, surf, birds, chimes, thunder), including the
  music: a soft, melancholic piano piece in A minor, composed on the fly so it never repeats, with taiko drums
  joining in when the challenge heats up.
- The world is hand-designed in `src/map.ts`.

See [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for the architecture and working rules.

## Development

- `G` opens the tuning panel: every flight, camera and visual constant as a live slider, plus readouts
  (fps, draw calls, triangles). Export JSON to make tuned values the new defaults.
- Add `#x,y,z,heading` to the URL to start anywhere (skips the title), e.g. `#-1900,500,2400,0` faces the volcano.
- Dev pages: `/dev/creature.html` (creature lab) and `/dev/models.html?file=architecture.glb&lod=0` (model viewer).
